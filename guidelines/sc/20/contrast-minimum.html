Contrast (Minimum)

AA

The visual presentation of text, images of text, and meaningful text included in non-text content has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, except for the following:

Large Text

Large-scale text, images of large-scale text, and large-scale text included in non-text content have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1.

Incidental

Text, images of text, and text included in non-text content that are part of an inactive user interface component,that are pure decoration, that are not visible to anyone, or that are an incidental part of a picture that contains significant other visual content and are not intended to convey important information, have no contrast requirement.

Logotypes

Text that is part of a logo or brand name has no contrast requirement.
